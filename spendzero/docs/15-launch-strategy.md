# Launch Strategy

## Pre-launch

- Closed beta (500–1000 invite-only users) sourced from personal-finance/
  no-spend communities, students, and people actively saving for a
  defined goal (trip, gadget, wedding) — the goal-allocation mechanic is
  the product's sharpest hook for this audience.
- Legal/trademark clearance on brand names (`legal-and-branding-safety.md`).
- Pre-written App Store/Play Store review notes explicitly stating: no
  real payment processing, no financial instrument storage, savings are
  self-reported tracking only — to pre-empt review friction given the
  "savings"/"financial wellness" framing.

## Positioning

"Every craving. Zero spending." Lead with the goal-allocation payoff, not
"shop without buying" alone — the differentiator versus a generic
shopping-simulator is that restraint visibly becomes *your* Goa
trip/iPhone/emergency fund, not just an abstract number.

## Channels

- Short-form video showing the "Craving Completed → I Saved It → goal
  bar fills" moment — the single most shareable beat in the product.
- Personal-finance creator partnerships (no-spend challenge, FIRE-India
  communities).
- Reddit/Discord frugality and savings-goal communities.
- ASO keywords: "no spend," "savings goal tracker," "shopping
  simulator," "stop impulse spending" — avoiding any real brand names.

## Phased rollout

1. Closed beta → MVP exit criteria gate (`12-mvp-scope.md`).
2. Open beta, 2–3 metro cities, staged capacity ramp.
3. Pan-India GA with Hindi localization live.
4. Paid acquisition only after organic D7 retention benchmark is hit.

## Monetization (explicitly non-transactional)

No real payments ever. Future candidates (V2+, evaluated separately):
non-intrusive sponsored fictional-brand placements, premium cosmetic
themes, or a freemium tier for advanced AI trip/goal planning — all
reviewed against store policy and the "no real money movement" promise
before implementation.
