# Our Purpose

Project Future exists to help people make everyday decisions that their future self will be grateful for.

This is not a finance app.

This is not a budgeting app.

This is not a shopping app.

This is not an expense tracker.

Project Future is an Intentional Living platform.

We exist to transform everyday temptations into meaningful progress toward the life people truly want.

---

# Why We Exist

Every day, millions of people make hundreds of financial decisions.

Some are intentional.

Most are automatic.

A coffee.

Late-night food.

An online sale.

A gadget.

A subscription.

A flash deal.

None of these purchases alone change someone's life.

But together, they silently shape it.

The problem isn't spending.

The problem is unconscious spending.

The modern world has become extraordinarily good at rewarding today's desires.

Very few products reward tomorrow's dreams.

Project Future exists to change that.

---

# The Problem

Today's world is optimized for dopamine.

Every platform competes for attention.

Every notification asks for action.

Every discount creates urgency.

Every delivery app reduces friction.

Every checkout is faster than reflection.

People are not weak.

People are surrounded by systems designed to remove thinking.

Project Future exists to restore one simple thing.

Pause.

That pause creates choice.

Choice creates intention.

Intention builds the future.

# Product Philosophy

Project Future is not trying to stop people from spending.

Project Future is helping people spend intentionally.

If buying something genuinely improves someone's life,

that purchase should be celebrated.

If a purchase only satisfies a temporary impulse,

the user should be encouraged to pause.

Not because spending is wrong.

Because tomorrow matters.

---

# North Star

Every product decision must answer

"Will this help the user make a decision their future self will be grateful for?"

If the answer is No,

the feature should not exist.

---

# Definition of Done

Every feature must pass all of these questions.

Does it strengthen the future?

Does it reinforce intentional living?

Does it create hope?

Does it celebrate progress?

Does it avoid guilt?

Does it feel premium?

Does it feel personal?

Does it align with our philosophy?

If any answer is No,

the feature is incomplete.
