# Privacy Policy — Project Future

_Last updated: 2026_

Project Future ("the app", "we") is a simulation-only intentional-spending
app. This policy explains what data the app handles. Host this page at a
public URL (e.g. a `/privacy` route on the web app) and enter that URL in the
Google Play Console.

## The short version
- We do **not** process payments, connect to your bank, or collect any card,
  UPI, or financial-account information. Ever.
- The app is a simulation: brands and listings are fictional, and your
  "savings" are a personal tally of purchases you chose not to make.
- We do not sell or share your data with third parties.
- We do not show ads.

## What we collect
To let you use the app without creating an account, we generate a random
**guest device identifier** and store the data you create against it:

- **Your goals / dreams** — titles, target amounts, dates, and progress you
  enter.
- **Your intentional-choice activity** — the tally of cravings you chose to
  skip and which goal you directed them toward.
- **A guest device identifier** — a random id generated by the app (not your
  device's hardware or advertising identifier) so your data syncs to you
  without a login.

We do **not** collect: your name (unless you type it into a goal), contacts,
location, photos, messages, real financial or payment information, or any
device advertising identifier.

## How we use it
Solely to provide the app's core function — saving and syncing your own
dreams and progress across sessions. We do not use your data for advertising
or profiling, and we do not share it with third parties.

## Data security
Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored on our
backend infrastructure.

## Data retention & deletion
Your data is retained while your guest profile is active. To request deletion
of your data, contact us at the email below and we will remove the data
associated with your guest identifier.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
data from them.
