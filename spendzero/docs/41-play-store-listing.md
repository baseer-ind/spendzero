## Full description (max 4000 chars)
```
Project Future turns everyday restraint into visible progress toward the life you want.

It isn't a budget tracker and it isn't a bank. It's a calm, beautiful space
that helps you pause before an impulse purchase — and redirect the money you
DON'T spend toward a dream you actually care about: a trip to Japan, a home,
a new laptop.

HOW IT WORKS
• Set a dream — where you want your money to take you.
• When a craving hits, open Project Future for a moment to choose.
• Skip the purchase, and the amount you resisted is recorded toward your dream.
• Watch your progress ring fill, your streak grow, and your future get closer.

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT
• Emotion, not spreadsheets. Every skipped craving becomes a small win you
  can see and feel.
• A gentle "one last pause" instead of guilt or restriction.
• A journey timeline of every intentional choice you've made.
• Designed to feel like a luxury journal, not a finance dashboard.

IMPORTANT — THIS IS A SIMULATION
Project Future does not process payments, connect to your bank, hold, move,
or invest any real money. The brands and listings in the app are fictional.
Your "savings" are a personal tally of purchases you chose not to make — a
motivation tool, not a financial product. No card, bank, or UPI details are
ever collected.

Start with one intention today. One more intentional day.
```

## Data safety form — drafted answers
- **Does the app collect or share user data?** Yes, collects (no sharing).
- **Data collected:**
  - *App activity* — the goals/dreams the user creates and their
    craving-session tally (stored on our backend to sync the user's own data).
  - *Device or other IDs* — a generated guest device id (not the hardware
    advertising id) used to identify the guest account without login.
- **Is data shared with third parties?** No.
- **Is data encrypted in transit?** Yes (HTTPS/TLS).
- **Can users request deletion?** Yes — provide a contact email / in-app path.
- **Financial info / payment info collected?** **No.** (Critical — the app
  never touches real money or payment instruments.)
- **Location, contacts, messages, photos, audio:** No.

## App access
