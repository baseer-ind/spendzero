# User Research Plan

An in-app, consent-based way to understand how people shop — to improve the
product and to personalise Future Intelligence later. **Not a diagnosis.**

## Framing

Titled "Help us understand how you shop" — never "assessment of your shopping
disorder" or any clinical language. Optional, skippable, revisitable.

## Question (multi-select)

"Which of these describe you?" (choose any):
- I browse to relax.
- I browse because I like discovering deals.
- I often buy things I didn't plan to buy.
- I usually compare prices.
- I use shopping apps mainly when I need something.
- I want to save more money.
- I want to understand my spending.
- I want to spend less time browsing.
- I enjoy browsing even when I don't buy.

Multiple answers allowed. Results map loosely to the three user types (Explore /
Future / "didn't know I had a pattern") — as *preferences*, not labels.

## Consent & storage

- **Separate consent** for any research use, stored apart from product data.
  Default: answers stay on-device (localStorage) and only shape in-app
  suggestions.
- If aggregate research use is ever introduced, it requires a distinct, explicit
  opt-in, anonymisation, and a stated retention period (DPDP Act alignment).
- No answer is required to use the app.

## Use in-product

- Tailor copy/entry points (e.g. deal-lovers → surface "The Discount Trap").
- Personalisation uses **observable behaviour + stated preference**, never a
  diagnosis, and never shaming.

## Status
Planned. The existing behavioural **Spending Profile** (onboarding assessment) is
separate and already shipped; this survey is a lighter, ongoing preference signal
and is not yet implemented.
