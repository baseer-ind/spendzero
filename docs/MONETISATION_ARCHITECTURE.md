# Monetisation Architecture — Project Future

> Two clearly separated buckets: **(A) technically possible now** (no licence needed) and
> **(B) requires regulatory / licensing / partner approval**. Nothing in B ships without legal review and a
> regulated partner. We never monetise in ways that conflict with the mission (no selling user data, no dark
> patterns, no pushing spending).

## Guiding principles
- The user's interest comes first; revenue must never require them to spend *more*.
- Transparency: any paid tier or partner referral is disclosed in-app.
- Privacy is a feature, not inventory: **behavioural profiles and personal data are never sold.**

---
## A. Technically possible now (no special licence)
These can be built on the existing product with standard app-store / SaaS mechanics.

1. **Premium subscription ("Future+").** Advanced insights, unlimited goals, richer future-self content,
   custom themes/covers, deeper journey analytics, data export, early features. Clear free core that
   delivers the mission; premium is enhancement, not a paywall on behaviour change.
   - Billing: Google Play Billing / RevenueCat. No extra licence.
2. **B2B / financial-wellness (white-label or seats).** Employers, universities, and credit unions buying
   Project Future as a wellbeing benefit. Aggregate, **anonymised, consented** outcome dashboards for the
   sponsor (never individual data). Strong, mission-aligned revenue.
3. **Affiliate / lead-generation to *non-regulated or self-regulated* products** (e.g., budgeting tools,
   courses). Must be clearly labelled, relevant, and never manipulative. (Note: referrals to *financial
   products* move to bucket B.)
4. **Content / partnerships** — branded "futures" or goal templates with ethical brands (fictional in-app
   catalogue stays fictional; brand tie-ins are explicit and optional).
5. **Anonymised, aggregated insight reports** (opt-in, privacy-preserving) about intentional-spending trends,
   sold to researchers/press — only if it never exposes individuals and users consent.

## B. Requires regulatory / licensing / partner approval
These are the **big** opportunities and the reason the domain is architected for real money — but each needs a
licensed partner and legal sign-off. **Do not build or advertise until then.**

1. **Real "Move to Savings" + referral economics.** When virtual Future Fund can move into a real
   partner-held account (bank / NBFC / regulated savings), revenue can come from a **referral / revenue-share
   with the regulated partner** for funded accounts — *not* from holding funds ourselves. (India: RBI-governed;
   partner is entity of record.)
2. **Round-ups / recurring deposits into a partner account.** Needs e-mandate + partner; potential rev-share.
3. **Interest / yield products or investing the saved amount.** Regulated (SEBI/advisory or bank). Only via a
   licensed partner; we facilitate UX, never advise or custody.
4. **Card / interchange** (a Project Future debit/prepaid card that auto-redirects "skipped" amounts). Needs a
   bank/EMI + card-network partner; interchange revenue. Significant compliance.
5. **Insurance / credit marketplace referrals.** Regulated referral arrangements (e.g., IRDAI/RBI-aligned).
   Lead-gen revenue, but licensed/partnered only.

---
## Sequencing (recommended)
1. **Now:** ship the behaviour-change product free; build trust + retention on the **virtual** model.
2. **Early revenue:** Future+ subscription + B2B financial-wellness (bucket A) — fund the company with no
   regulatory exposure.
3. **Horizon:** sign a regulated savings partner → launch real "Move to Savings" → partner revenue-share
   (bucket B). This is the moat and the step-change in both retention and revenue.

## Hard "never" list
- Never sell or share individual user data or behaviour profiles.
- Never earn by making users spend more or by dark patterns.
- Never imply the app holds/insures funds or gives financial advice without the required licences/partners.
- Never present bucket-B features as available before the partner + licences exist.
